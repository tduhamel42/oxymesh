# Security

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Report it privately instead: **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**.

Useful to include:
- the version (commit) and the device;
- what an attacker needs: over the air, a paired app, or physical access;
- how to reproduce it: the bytes, a command, or a fuzz input.

In scope:
- the decoders of over-the-air packets and of companion-protocol commands;
- encryption and signatures;
- Bluetooth pairing;
- what the firmware keeps in flash.
