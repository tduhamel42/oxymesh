# Elecrow ThinkNode M5: hardware board

Source of truth for `firmware/esp32s3/src/board/m5.rs`. Sources:
- MeshCore v1.18 (`variants/thinknode_m5/*`, MIT) — marked **MC**;
- Meshtastic variant `ELECROW-ThinkNode-M5` (commit 9bcf0f7, GPL: facts only, ADR-0007) — marked **MT**;
- to be checked on the device in MC4 where the two disagree or say nothing — marked **?**.

## 1. Microcontroller

ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N4: 4 MB flash, **no PSRAM**, 240 MHz, dual core. Native USB unused: the USB-C connector goes through a **CH340** bridge (USB `1a86:7522`) to **UART0** (TX 43, RX 44) (MT). On macOS, the WCH driver is needed (port `/dev/cu.wchusbserial*`).

GPIO19/20 (native USB D−/D+) are used for the GPS UART: native USB is not possible.

Pitfalls:
- GPIO45 and GPIO46 are strapping pins.

## 3. Battery

Open-circuit voltage curve (MT), in mV, from 100 % to 0 % in 10 % steps:
`4100, 4050, 3990, 3890, 3800, 3720, 3630, 3530, 3420, 3300, 3100`.

Percentage by linear interpolation between two points, moving average over 8 readings to avoid jumps. No reliable reading while charging: we show the charging symbol (GPIO12) and the last value. Multiplier calibrated in MC4 against a voltmeter.

## 4. Power and sleep

- At boot: IO4 (peripherals) driven high, GPIO46 (LoRa) driven high, notification LED off.
- Hibernation: screen in deep sleep, radio asleep, GPS off, backlight off, IO4 low, then ESP32 deep sleep with **wake-up on the top button (GPIO14)**. (The C++ hibernates with no wake-up source.)
- The C++ companion never sleeps; light sleep will come later, measured.

## 5. Clock, noise floor, chip temperature

- **Clock**: the PCF8563 keeps UTC, like the C++ (`AutoDiscoverRTCClock` with RTClib's `RTC_PCF8563`: years 2000-2099, century bit unused), so either firmware reads what the other wrote. At boot its time is taken unless the chip lost power (VL bit) or reads before 2026-11-05; otherwise the clock starts at that date until a phone or the GPS sets it. Every `SET_DEVICE_TIME` (and later a GPS fix) writes it back (`Action::ClockSet`).
- **Noise floor**: the C++ sampler (`RadioLibWrappers.cpp:62-105`, `mc_core::noise`): every 2 s a round of 64 instantaneous RSSI readings while listening with no preamble or header detected; a reading counts below floor + 14 dB; the floor is their mean, never below −120 dBm, and 0 until the first round ends. Shown on Home and in Signal, sent in `GET_STATS`.
- **Chip temperature**: the ESP32-S3's internal sensor, read every 10 s for device telemetry (the C++ sends it too). esp-hal 1.2.2 has no driver for the S3: the documented SENS fields are used (power-up and dump-out under software control), with the factory calibration from eFuse `TEMP_CALIB`. The range DAC, behind the analog I2C bus that esp-hal keeps private, stays at its reset value, taken as the −10 to 80 °C range (°C = 0.4386 × raw − 20.52 − calibration). Checked against the Heltec V4 (ESP32-S3, C++).
